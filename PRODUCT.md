# PRODUCT — J&J Garage Doors LLC

## Platform
- Platform: web
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), GitHub Pages, no frameworks, no tracking

## Users
- Homeowners and business owners in New Jersey, Pennsylvania and New York with a garage door that is stuck, off track, noisy, or has a broken spring, or who want a new door or opener.
- Many find the link on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser. Many are Spanish speakers (the business posts in Spanish; inferred that a large share of customers prefer Spanish).
- The owner (Jimmy Rodriguez) sees the site first; it has to look like his business, done right.

## Purpose
- Get the visitor to call or text J&J about their door, with the problem already described.
- Show that J&J is a real, family-owned, trilingual crew that does repairs, service, installation and sales, residential and commercial.
